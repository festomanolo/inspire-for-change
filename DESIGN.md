# DESIGN.md — Inspired for Change (Church Events & Sanctuary Operations)

## Visual Direction
- **Archetype**: Architectural Sanctuary Operations (Mineral Stone & Sanctuary Cypress)
- **Anti-Slop Guardrails**: Enforced via `pbakaus/impeccable` + `Nutlope/hallmark` Floor + `design-taste-frontend`
  - Zero `Inter` or overused LLM defaults; uses **Figtree** for architectural UI/display and **IBM Plex Mono** (`tabular-nums`) for UUIDs, badge codes (`IFC-26-0001`), phone numbers, and counts.
  - Zero purple-to-blue gradients; calibrated OKLCH tokens at `:root` and `.dark` (`--primary` deep sanctuary cypress `oklch(0.30 0.065 168)`, `--warm` hospitality terracotta `oklch(0.52 0.135 46)`).
  - Zero emoji icons; standardized `@phosphor-icons/react` vector iconography.
  - Double-bezel (`bezel-shell` + `bezel-core`) machined enclosures on primary operational surfaces.
  - GPU-only motion (`transform`, `opacity`) with `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` and `:focus-visible` keyboard rings.
